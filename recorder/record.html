Record the 26 letter sounds

Before you start: use a quiet room and hold the microphone about a hand's width from your mouth. Say the sound the letter makes, not its name (for B say "buh", not "bee"). Keep stops (b, d, g, k, p, t) very short and crisp, and hold the hissing and humming sounds (f, l, m, n, r, s, v, z) for about one second. Everything stays on this computer.
